RETURN POLICY
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. To start a return, go to "My Orders" and select "Return Item". Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

SHIPPING POLICY
Standard shipping takes 4-7 business days and is free on orders above Rs. 499. Express shipping (1-3 business days) costs Rs. 149. We currently ship across India but do not offer international shipping.

WARRANTY POLICY
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects. Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs.

CANCELLATION POLICY
Orders can be cancelled for free anytime before they are shipped. Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled but can be returned after delivery as per the return policy. To cancel, go to "My Orders" and select "Cancel Order".

EXCHANGE POLICY
Exchanges are allowed within 15 days of delivery, only for size or color variants of the same product, subject to availability. Exchange requests can be raised from the order details page. There is no additional fee for exchanges.

PAYMENT AND REFUNDS
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders under Rs. 5000. Refunds for prepaid orders are credited to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Refunds for COD orders are issued via bank transfer or store credit, whichever the customer chooses.
